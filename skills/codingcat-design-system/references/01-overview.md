# 01. Overview: Brand Ethos & Core Identity

## Brand Identity
- **Brand Name**: CodingCat.dev
- **Tagline**: "Purrfect Web Tutorials"
- **Founder & Host**: Alex Patterson (`@alex.patterson`), Senior Solutions Engineer, Google Developer Expert (GDE), and AWS Community Builder.
- **Mascot**: **AJ the Cat**, an anthropomorphic black coding cat with large expressive Electric Lime Green (`#C5E86C`) eyes, a Soft Lavender (`#B4A7D6`) collar with a pastel rainbow hexagonal `"AJ"` tag, and over-ear Royal Purple/Lavender headphones (`"CodingCat.dev Podcast"` across the headband) that properly cover his ears.

## Aesthetic Philosophy
CodingCat.dev is **dark-mode-first**, built for high-contrast developer ergonomics while remaining playful, welcoming, and deeply technical:
1. **Crisp Modern Dark UI**: Built on Canvas Obsidian (`#0F172A`) and Surface Slate (`#1E293B`) with tactile Royal Purple (`#4B2E83`), Soft Lavender (`#B4A7D6`), and Electric Lime Green (`#C5E86C`) accents.
2. **Chunky Display + Clean Technical Hierarchy**: Pairs friendly extra-bold `Nunito` (`800`/`900`) `H1` display titles with crisp `Inter` (`H2`-`H6` and prose), `JetBrains Mono` (code and metadata badges), and hand-drawn `Virgil` / `Excalifont` diagram annotations.
3. **Hand-Drawn Dark-Mode Excalidraw Architecture**: Technical concepts are explained visually using pure black (`#000000`) or deep slate (`#0F172A`) Excalidraw canvases with white (`#FFFFFF`) and lavender (`#B4A7D6`) sketchy strokes, Mint (`#86EFAC`) or Lime (`#C5E86C`) checkmarks for recommended patterns, and Coral (`#FCA5A5`) `X` marks for anti-patterns.

## When to Use AJ the Mascot vs. Alex Patterson

| Context | Primary Subject | Visual & Editorial Guidance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Graphics & Empty States** | **AJ Mascot** | Use 2D vector AJ (`assets/AJHeadphones.png` or `assets/AJ-2D-Bipedal.jpg`) for site emblems, 404 states, badges, and stickers. |
| **Callout & Pro-Tip Boxes** | **AJ Mascot** | Pair AJ's head emblem with a left-border accent (`#C5E86C` or `#B4A7D6`) for quick architectural callouts. |
| **3D Bumper Animations & Toys** | **AJ Mascot (3D)** | Use the 3D vinyl-toy turnaround renders (`assets/AJ-3D-Headphones-Front.jpg`, `Left`, `Right`) for video bumpers and hero showcases. |
| **YouTube Thumbnails (16:9)** | **AJ Mascot or Alex Patterson** | Place a high-contrast cutout of AJ reacting to a diagram/logo, or a die-cut cutout of Alex Patterson (and podcast guests) in the left or right third. |
| **Tutorial Narration & Opinions** | **Alex Patterson** | First-person architectural takes, "What the F*ck is..." explainers, DevRel event cards, and presenter camera cutaways always feature Alex Patterson directly. |
| **Excalidraw Diagram Canvas** | **Separate from Presenter** | Keep `@alex.patterson` camera cutaways separate from the Excalidraw diagram canvas so architecture labels remain unobstructed. |
